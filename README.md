# Iran War Market Ledger

An interactive, bilingual (English / فارسی) timeline of the Iran–US/Israel war (June 2025 onward) set beside market data: US Treasury yields, the S&P 500, oil, ship traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, prediction-market odds. Every event cites its sources, claims are attributed to whoever made them, and the page shows where the two sides disagree without deciding who is right.

**Live site:** https://aminkvh.github.io/iran-war-market-ledger/
